Backup & restore

Download a copy of your data, or bring one back from an earlier download. Snapshots of the data folder (for example a TrueNAS snapshot task) are the other way to back up.

Download a backup

Hold Ctrl (Cmd on a Mac) to choose several.

Downloads a ZIP with the data and the PDF files; without them, a JSON file.

Choose Check contents to see what will be included.

Restore from a backup

A .zip (data and PDFs) or .json (data only) downloaded from this page.

How to restore
Patients, providers and tests with the same name are matched; results are added.
Everything here is deleted first, then the backup is restored.

Refuse files that aren't a complete MyLabVault backup.

This changes your data. Download a backup first if you might want to go back.

Clean up duplicates

Find lab tests, panels, units and providers saved more than once (for example Albumin and Albumin (g/dL)), review each suggestion, and merge the ones you choose. Results move to the item you keep. Nothing changes until you confirm.

The AI review sends the names of your tests, panels, units and providers (not your results) to Claude in Amazon Bedrock.
You'll see a summary to confirm before anything is merged.

Danger zone

Reset all data

Permanently deletes every result, vital, medication, vaccine, test, panel, unit, provider, patient, uploaded PDF and setting, then starts again with the welcome screen. This can't be undone. Download a backup first.

Confirm merges

These items will be merged. Everything that uses a merged item moves to the one you keep, and the merged items are deleted. This can't be undone; export your data first if you want a backup.
